What's needed to start building

  1. Pick a direction: A, B or C, or a mix (for example A's colours with B's leaf pattern).
  2. Approve the sitemap: 15 pages in Bahasa Malaysia and English. The gallery waits until there are photos with parental consent, and transport moves onto the Admissions page.
  3. OK to install Node.js LTS (free) on the build machine.

Photos above are drawn placeholders. The real site uses only photos the centre supplies, and no identifiable child without written parental consent.
